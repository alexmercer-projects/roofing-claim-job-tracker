# Roofing claim job tracker

A sample Excel workbook for tracking roofing insurance-claim jobs, plus a short leave-behind that explains the usual next steps to a homeowner.

This is a concept sample. The homeowners, addresses, carriers, claim numbers, and dollar amounts in the workbook are labeled SAMPLE and are not real. The leave-behind is general information, not legal or insurance advice.

## What's in the folder

- `Roofing-Claim-Job-Tracker-SAMPLE.xlsx` — Dashboard, Jobs, and a How to use tab. The first few job rows are fictional placeholders. Formulas cover counts, 72-hour rescission windows, balances, and days since last touch.
- `Hail-Claim-Homeowner-Leave-Behind-SAMPLE.md` — A plain-language page a contractor could hand to a homeowner after a storm inspection. It mentions a few Colorado consumer rules that are already on the books; it is not a substitute for a lawyer or an insurance agent.

Open the workbook in Excel or another spreadsheet app that can read `.xlsx`. There is no site to run.
